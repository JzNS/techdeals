The power bank guide

Capacity is the headline number, but output wattage and travel rules decide whether a power bank is useful or dead weight.

mAh: what the number actually means
  • 10,000 mAh ≈ two to three phone charges.
  • 20,000 mAh also tops up a tablet or small laptop.
  • The usable amount is roughly 65–70% of the printed figure — that's energy-conversion physics, not a scam.
Output wattage matters more than capacity

A big bank stuck at 12W will charge your phone slowly all day. Look for 20W or higher if you want real fast charging, and ideally a USB-C port that does Power Delivery both in and out.

Travel rules (flights)
  • Hand luggage only — power banks are not allowed in checked baggage.
  • Up to 100 Wh needs no airline permission. That's about 27,000 mAh at 3.7V, so every model we list qualifies comfortably.
