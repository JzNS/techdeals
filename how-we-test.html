How we pick products

Short answer: we look at what people actually buy, read the complaints first, and keep only the models that survive. Here is the process in full.

The four filters

  1. Real demand. We start from best-seller lists and review volume. A product with a few hundred honest reviews tells us more than one with thousands of suspiciously perfect stars.
  2. The complaints first. We read the 1- and 3-star reviews before anything else. Repeated problems — cables that stop charging, earbuds that fall out, power banks that lose capacity after a month — are instant disqualifiers.
  3. Specs against price. A 100W cable and a 240W cable are not the same purchase. We make sure each product is good for what it costs, not just “the best” in its class.
  4. Honest fit. If a cheaper option does the same job for most people, we say so instead of pushing the pricier one.

What we don't do

We don't accept payment to feature or rank a product. We have no stock and no customer service — every order is placed directly on Amazon, which means their buyer protection, Prime delivery and returns apply.
