Buying guide

The earbud guide

Specs on the box rarely match the experience in your ear. Here's what to look at before you buy.

Fit beats everything else

No codec or chip can fix earbuds that don't seal the ear canal. If tips keep falling out, get a size set (most come with S/M/L). A proper seal gives more bass and is what makes noise cancellation actually work.

Bluetooth version
  • 5.3 or newer = stabler connection, lower lag for video and games.
  • If you game on a phone or watch streaming content often, this is the spec to prioritise over raw battery.
Noise cancellation (ANC)

Genuinely worth it for trains, planes and open-plan offices. If you mostly listen at home on a couch, skip paying extra for it — the difference is small there.

Battery: read between the numbers

Advertised hours always assume full volume off and no ANC. Real-world time per charge is usually 6–7 h for a good set, with another full charge or two from the case.

For sport: look for at least IPX4. Sweat — not drops — is the main reason cheap earbuds die early.
